# Pico brand reference

Keep the product presentation concise and consistent across the website, GitHub, launch material and social profiles.

## Name and purpose
- **Name:** Pico. (include the final period as part of the wordmark; never space it as `Pico .`).
- **Category:** Browser-based Computer Science learning tool.
- **One-liner:** Write code, build flowcharts and simulate digital logic and CPU execution in your browser.
- **Short description:** Pico combines a pseudocode compiler, Python compiler, flowchart builder, logic simulator and CPU Studio in one free, account-free learning environment.

## Visual system
- **Logo:** Use `public/pico-logo.svg` as the source. Do not stretch, crop or replace it with unrelated symbols.
- **Signature color:** `#8179ef` (logo purple). Respect user-selected app themes inside workspaces.
- **Dark backdrop for branded media:** `#1e1e2e`. Prefer restrained, high-contrast, accessible combinations.
- **Typography:** Prioritize legibility, consistent hierarchy and the existing app fonts. Don't introduce extra fonts into individual workspaces only for decoration.
- **Spacing:** Keep the brand icon separate from the entire `Pico.` wordmark; the period stays attached to `Pico`.

## Voice
- Direct, confident and useful. Technical wording must be accurate about what each workspace actually simulates.
- Humor may appear in the custom 404, but not in consent, errors, privacy explanations or educational instructions.
- Avoid describing educational CPU timing as real hardware GHz, or simulated instructions as real CPU machine code.

## Ownership and release
Keep the existing Authors page and contributions accurate. New launch material should be reviewed by the team; do not imply endorsement by Cambridge, Pearson, a school, or another organization without permission.
